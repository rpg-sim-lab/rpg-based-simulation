## Measurement choices (approved together with the kit shas; none changes a rule)
1. **Night is one instrument for both arms:** `is_night` from the NEW tree's `src/core/calendar.py`, loaded by file path (its sha256 is written into the summary), applied to the row's `world_time` (the world clock the state carries, not the tick) of an engagement's first attack. Night is TWO bounds (`tod >= 1800 or tod < 600`); the kit re-derives nothing. BASE's own simulation had no `is_night`; BASE's day/night split is the shared instrument's view, not what BASE's agents used.
2. **Periods table**, keyed by species id, the same for both arms, **pinned inside `person_attack_summary.py`** (`PERIODS`, `PERIODS_QUOTE`, `PERIODS_SOURCE`, `MISSING_LAW_QUOTE`, so the kit sha256 covers it; a test compares the quote with the rule text) from the owner amendment Q5(a) (`survival-needs.md:1042-1044` at 87792e3e8): diurnal = human, elf, dwarf, orc, lizardfolk, small_plant_eater, dragonkin; nocturnal = goblin, wolf, spider, undead, troll; any hour = slime, spirit. A species not in the table is never defaulted: its engagements are `period_unknown` and counted (choice 9).
3. **Inside the active period:** diurnal = not night, nocturnal = night, any hour = always; evaluated at the engagement's first attack. This is the kit's reading of SURV-08.
4. **Engagement:** attack events (hit or miss) by one attacker on one victim; gap <= 50 ticks stays, gap > 50 opens a new one (controls at 50 and 51). A **miss** is an update with `combat.attacker_id` set and `hp_delta >= 0`; **a hit** is `hp_delta < 0`, the ledger's own definition.
5. **Outcome of an engagement**, first match: `victim_killed`, `victim_fled` (movement mode RETREAT after an event, or a RETREAT mode row within the engagement plus the gap), `attacker_fled`, `unresolved_at_end` (the run ended within the gap), else `disengaged`.
6. **R3** is evaluated on the tighter reading: most engagements one attacker STARTS inside any rolling 2400-tick window (the calendar-day bin maximum is reported too).
7. **R7** reads the O3-group hits (the attackers the trace is about); the same counts over every attacker are reported as `all_attackers_info`. A flight or fight DECISION between two hits of one victim: its mode after the first hit is RETREAT (flight) or PURSUE (fight), or it attacked on that tick (fight), or a mode row RETREAT/PURSUE lies between the two ticks, or its mode before the second hit is RETREAT/PURSUE. Asleep = the task action is in `SLEEP_ACTIONS`; lodged = `navigation.lodged_in` set; both read from the state BEFORE the hit. **Amended by rpg-designer's ruling CHANGES on 5a21dcae4 (2026-11-22), C1 and C2:** a chain of consecutive hits on one victim resets when two consecutive hits are more than 50 ticks apart (the engagement gap), so one chain never spans two engagements; and the spy records `attack_rows` (tick, attacker id, victim id) for every attack BY a watched id on any target, hit or miss, and each is a fight decision of that id, so a person fighting back is not "no flight or fight decision".
8. **R1** checks the attacker's group against its declared diet read a second time straight from the kind's content (`get_kind_properties(species).body.diet`): a miss is a metric-grouping finding for testing.
9. **Applicability (rpg-designer's pre-data ruling, relayed at rpg-planner-ubuntu's seat):** no seed is exempt below 10 engagements. R1 (holds by construction, still checked and reported), R3, R4 and R7 are judged in EVERY seed at any count; R6 in every seed with >= 10 NEW hits; R2 and R5 are share rules and read `NOT_EVALUATED (n<10)` below 10 engagements (with the value they would have had in `if_applied`). If no NEW seed reaches 10 engagements the run line reads "R2/R5 NOT EVALUATED (n<10)" and R1, R3, R4, R6, R7 decide (my earlier whole-run NOT EVALUATED reading is dropped).
10. **Arms and attribution (rpg-designer's answer, fixed logic, no switch):** R1-R5 and R7 are judged on NEW; BASE is context, computed and shown side by side. A miss on NEW AND on BASE in the same seed reads "BUG CANDIDATE, pre-existing (not attributed to #953/#954)"; a miss on NEW only reads "BUG CANDIDATE, attributed". R6 is NEW vs BASE (a miss is attributed). Every miss names the attacker ids.
11. **Not assessable is not a pass:** in a seed with >= 10 engagements, if the engagements of unknown-species attackers are >= 10% of the seed's engagements, R5 reads `NOT_CLEARED (R5 not assessable)`, which counts as HELD, with the species listed. **Ruled by rpg-designer (confirmed in the follow-up to the answers):** below 10%, the share is reported with the species named and R5 decides on the assessed engagements only (>= 90% of them inside the active period); at >= 10% the seed is NOT CLEARED (HELD). Every R5 miss carries the tag "SURV-08 law MISSING in code". An invalid cell (twin hashes differ, wiring mismatch, ...) on NEW cannot be cleared either: HELD.
12. **Overall verdict:** any miss on NEW -> BUG CANDIDATE (attributed if any miss is attributed, else pre-existing; HELD items are still listed); else any NOT_CLEARED, unassessable or invalid NEW cell -> HELD (HELD outranks CLEARED); else **CLEARED**, which needs R3, R4, R6 and R7 to hold in every seed and R2 and R5 wherever they are evaluated. The night/day split is labelled a MEASUREMENT in the output (`measurement_labels`): NEW's `is_night` applied offline to both arms; BASE's agents never used it.
13. **Recording OFF:** like the births re-check, both the twin and the spy run with `SIM_OBS_MODE=OFF`, `RPG_OBS_MODE=OFF` and the `no_replay` kernel flag; the spy drives the REAL `RunLedger.observe` itself (events discarded). The gate's own runs record ON; the sim is deterministic, so the O3 counts should equal the gate's (a second-source check: NEW FLW s42..s46 = 51, 5, 0, 59, 5 and BASE 0, 0, 0, 3, 0 in the brief), but that equality is a check, not an assumption.

14. **Group scope (ruled by rpg-designer; the extension is rpg-planner-ubuntu's):** R1-R7 and the verdict apply to attackers whose kind group is exactly a member of `TIED_GROUPS = ("meat",)` in `person_attack_summary.py` (the brief's "FLW meat O3.attacks_on_people" row, 0.6 -> 24.0; O3 rows are keyed by the exact group, `rpg_gate_metrics.py:396-411`; FLW `meat` is the only O3 row in all 20 saved gate reports and v2, so the constant has no other member today). Each tied group is judged separately and its R1 reads "every counted attacker is in that exact group (and its declared diet is that group's classes)". Every other O3 group is reported IN FULL under `groups` (engagements, attackers, pairs, hit states, the rules as "context, no verdict") and never changes a verdict.
15. **Disk floor (ruled by rpg-designer, C3):** at least 2 GiB free on the record set's filesystem (OUT) AND on `/`. `person_attack_run.disk_stop` (a stubbed free-space function in the controls) is called by the launcher before the start (an ABORT) and before EVERY cell (a clean stop: `STATUS=STOPPED_DISK_FLOOR_BEFORE_<cell>`, exit 4, the summarizer does not run, the seal still does). The stop record is `<OUT>/disk_floor_stop.json` (floor, free bytes per path, the cell it stopped before) and a line in `launcher.log`; if even that file cannot be written the launcher log carries `record_not_written`. A stop is not a pass.

## What the spy cross-checks inside every cell (cell-invalidating)
**Ledger liveness (added after the first preflight, where the ledger counted nothing and every spy cell exited 5):** `finish()` now runs BEFORE `kernel.shutdown()` (`run_ticks`), a ledger found switched off before an observe is switched on again and the tick recorded (`ledger_reasserts`), and the document carries the evidence: `_live`/`_header_sent` at the first observe and at finish (private reads, evidence only), `ledger.observe` calls against `apply_calls`, the ledger's public `first_tick`/`last_tick` against the spy's own. A ledger not live at finish, or reset after the spy started, fails loudly as `LEDGER_NOT_LIVE` (exit 7), distinct from a counting mismatch (exit 5). The cause of the first preflight's empty ledger is NOT proven (the diagnosis "finish after shutdown" is a candidate; the alternative is a kernel reset after `start_ledger()`); the rerun's evidence block settles it.

The spy's count of O3-group hit rows must equal the ledger's own `attacks` counter summed the way `_combat_rows` sums it, per group and in total (`WIRING_MISMATCH`, exit 5, else); the global observability mode is re-asserted OFF on every apply; the twin's and the spy's final hashes must be equal; the pinned-runner verdict of both must be PINNED; no ERROR+ log line.
