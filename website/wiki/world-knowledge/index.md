# World Knowledge

This space is for anyone watching a world run who wants to know what they are seeing. A world runs by itself: nobody plays it and nothing is scripted.

Every page here is built from the repository's own records each time the site is built. The wiki adds layout only. To change what a page says, change the record named at the bottom of that page.

## The sections

- **[Laws of the world](/wiki/world-knowledge/laws)** are the rules every world follows, grouped by area. Each rule has an ID, a name and a short statement.
- **[How the world runs](/wiki/world-knowledge/mechanisms)** lists the mechanisms that move the world, from one being up to the whole world. It shows how often each layer runs and which mechanisms are active.
- **[Who and what is in the world](/wiki/world-knowledge/catalog)** holds tables of the beings, groups, factions, places, items and recipes a world is built from.

## Exact formulas

Exact numbers and formulas are not on this site. They live in the [Mechanics Bible](https://github.com/ttnhan18062000/rpg-based-simulation/tree/main/docs/mechanics) in the repository.
