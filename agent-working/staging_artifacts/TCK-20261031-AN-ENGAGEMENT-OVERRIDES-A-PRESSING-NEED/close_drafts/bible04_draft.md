**A pressing need outranks every goal and act that is not acting on the subject (SURV-07 LAW P, memo row 88, shape S1 with the hunt exemption, TCK-20261031-AN-ENGAGEMENT-OVERRIDES-A-PRESSING-NEED).** At every decision site that chooses between a need past its escalation onset (`need_pull.need_is_pressing`, 0.6 of its line) and any other goal or act, the need wins unless a hostile ACTS on the subject (`need_yields`, `src/engine/tactical_threat.py`; `acting_threat_to` in `src/ai/goals/present_threat.py` is the same predicate over the subject's perceived hostiles). A hostile that is merely seen, adjacent, closing without a task or outmatching is a state, not an act, and does not hold a pressing need back. In the tactical pass this puts the need path (in-place survival, then the hunger or fatigue project's objective step, `src/engine/tactical_objective.py`) ahead of the presence test, role obligation, cohesion and target selection, so a follower with a pressing need takes no part in the group's shared target unless the target is acting on that follower; a subject whose project is not a need project does not engage a non-acting hostile (it waits for the brain, which a pressing need wakes). The in-place rest, eat-carried and eat-at-corpse steps keep AGENCY-07's `present_threat_terms` (shape S1); a fatigue arrival under the need path dispatches REST only when it would lodge or those terms are clear. Below the onset nothing changes, and whether to flee at all stays on AGENCY-07's `present_threat_to`.
**The hunt is the need's way (rpg-designer's ruling).** With HUNGER pressing, a prey in sight (`Relation.PREDATION`, the test the hunt opening step uses) and no hostile acting on the subject, the need path does not hold the subject back from the prey: it skips the need-first branch and takes its target from the prey only, so a hostile that is not prey and not acting is still not engaged. The exemption does not apply to a subject with an active need project whose current objective names no living target (an inn, a shop, work, a forage node, a carcass or a bed); that walk is its way and keeps the need path. A hunger or fatigue project that names a living target in the prey list is not suspended by that prey; a COMBAT_ENGAGE project is not a need project and keeps its suspension. Sleep alone pressing carries no exemption.
