P1-a (count any pending ENTITY_ACT regardless of payload) is REJECTED: it would also stop EAT / REST steps with no evidence that those steps are wrong. Why the as-worded P1 (a pending action task "with a payload") cannot be used: the actions phase runs before movement routing (pipeline.py:299 then :301) and empties a successful HOLD's payload (pipeline_phases/actions.py:317-328).
- **P2 (the gap).** The typed waits clear the stored target in the same update that sets HOLD: add `target_clear=True` to the `NavigationUpdate` of WATCH_HOSTILE (src/engine/tactical_hold.py:105-109), AVOID_NO_ROUTE (:114-118) and HOLD_CHOKEPOINT (src/engine/tactical.py:620-626). Measured: the next decision never re-chose the same target (0 of 17). Known limit (investigation 9, C2): the objective path re-derives its target at the next strategic pass (cadence 10).
- **P3-alt (the lifecycle).**
  - **Typed waits:** HOLD stays until the next decision that SETS A WALK; that decision resets the mode explicitly (WANDER, `NavigationComponent` default, state.py:494) in the same update that sets the target, at each mode-less target writer (list below), **but only once HOLD has outlived its entering task (the guard below)**. A wait that ends with no walk decision keeps HOLD and nothing steps; that is correct.
  - **Kill and collapse:** reset to WANDER when their task ends, in the task-end branch of the actions phase (pipeline_phases/actions.py:317-328): if the ending action is not `HOLD` and the effective mode is HOLD, add `movement_mode_set=MovementMode.WANDER` to the same update. Kill ends when the next swing at the dead target fails (TARGET_INCAPACITATED, actions.py:89-107); collapse ends at wake (`_survival_action_ends`, :51-58). A HOLD-action success (the typed waits) must NOT reset there. `_hold_in_place` (actions.py:77-80) and `collapse_update` (sleep_debt.py:67-73) need no change.
  - **REJECTED:** the one-site version where apply turns any mode-less `target_set` into a reset (an implicit coupling in the patch).

## GUARD (designer, v2 change): a walk-decision reset NEVER ends a HOLD whose entering task is still live
Reason: the MOVE_TO at action_intent.py:139 is the writer that walks collapsed sleepers with a live SLEEP task. Today the 10x HOLD price plus the reroute rung holds them to 12 steps on fm; a HOLD->WANDER reset at :139 would let them walk at cost 20 and make the sleeper bug worse. Kill and collapse holds end only with their task (the actions-phase branch above). In practice the walk-decision reset therefore fires only for a HOLD that has OUTLIVED its entering task, i.e. the typed waits. The guard applies to ALL SIX reset writers (intelligence.py:649, :670; redirection.py:97, :128; tactical_hold.py:99 KEEP_WALKING; intent/action_intent.py:139).
- **One shared helper** (a small pure function, e.g. `hold_reset_mode(entity, ent_upd)` in src/engine/, used by all six writers, so no writer carries its own copy of the rule): it returns `MovementMode.WANDER` when the effective mode is HOLD AND no action task is live, else None (no mode written). Effective mode = the pending update's `navigation.movement_mode_set` if present, else `entity.navigation.movement_mode`.
- **How a live entering task is detected, and why it is typed state and not a reason string:** the helper reads only `TaskComponent.work_kind` and the truthiness of `TaskComponent.payload` (the same predicate as `MovementCandidateSelector.holds_action_task`, candidate_selector.py:227-234: `work_kind == "ENTITY_ACT" and bool(payload)`), and, when the tick's pending update carries a `TaskUpdate`, that update's `work_kind_set` and the truthiness of `payload_set` (an update that empties the payload has ENDED the task this tick, as the actions phase does for a successful HOLD, actions.py:317-328). It never looks at `payload["reason"]` or `payload["action"]`. Both holds qualify: the collapse SLEEP is `TaskUpdate(work_kind_set="ENTITY_ACT", payload_set={"action": "SLEEP", COLLAPSE_FLAG: True})` (sleep_debt.py:67-73) and stays non-empty each tick until `_survival_action_ends` empties it at wake (actions.py:51-58, :328); the killing ATTACK keeps its annotated, non-empty payload (actions.py:330-331) until the next swing at the dead target fails and the task ends with `{}` (actions.py:89-107, :328). A typed wait's task is emptied in its own tick (actions.py:317-328), so it is NOT live and the reset may fire.
- **Limit, said plainly:** the predicate is "an action task is live", not "THE task that entered the HOLD is live": the state does not store which task entered the HOLD. It errs toward keeping HOLD: a typed-wait HOLD that persists while the subject has since started an unrelated live action (e.g. an ATTACK) is not reset by a walk decision until that action ends. That is the safe direction (it never makes a walk cheaper than today), and it costs at most the time until that action ends.

Two further things seen while listing (for the designer; not part of the build unless ruled): (1) `NavigationUpdate` has TWO clear flags: `target_clear` (applied: patches.py:284) and `clear_target` (never applied to state; only read by actor_validity.py:76 and set at actor_validity.py:116, movement.py:413 `clear_target=replan` and blacksmith.py:250), so those three writers do not clear the stored target; P1-b and P2 use `target_clear` on purpose. (2) a same-tick wait (target_clear) merged with a same-tick strategic target write: the order of the merge decides; a test pins it.
